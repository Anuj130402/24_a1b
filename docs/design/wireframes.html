CareConnect — wireframes

Structure and data flow for the six screens. Deliberately unstyled — colours and components come from the design system. Endpoints reference docs/api_endpoints.md.

Who uses this

Three roles, switched from a dropdown in the top bar. Real login is out of scope.

Front-desk coordinator

Works at a clinic reception. Handles patients arriving for consultations.

  • Look up a patient and check their HSA balance before booking
  • Book an appointment and collect the copay
  • Move patients through waiting → consultation → discharged
  • Answer "where did my money go?" from the ledger
Browse · Book · Audit

Clinic administrator

Oversees several clinics. Cares about revenue trends and patient sentiment.

  • See whether a clinic's revenue is rising or falling
  • Compare clinics against each other on a given day
  • Check monthly discharge counts
  • Read what patients say about care quality
Analytics · Reviews

Dispatch coordinator

Sends mobile nurses to patients who cannot travel.

  • Pick a location and see which nurses are nearby
  • Know how far away each one is
  • Watch positions update as nurses move

Applies to every screen

·Loading, empty and error are mandatory. No blank pages — it's an explicit grading line. The page template in the starter kit has all three branches already.
·Actor dropdown in the top bar switches between the three roles. It only changes which records are in scope; there's no authentication.
·Sidebar collapses to a horizontal scroller under 760px. Laptop properly, phone reasonably.
·Status changes happen from Browse, on the appointment detail view — that's how you demonstrate the partial unique index: discharge, then book the same patient again.
